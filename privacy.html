Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Jarvis is a private assistant operated by Jarvis Labs for its owner only. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign up or message it.

What data Jarvis processes

  • WhatsApp messages exchanged between the owner and Jarvis's business number, including the phone number and message content.
  • Calendar entries, notes, tasks and meeting summaries that the owner has connected, read only to prepare messages for the owner.

How the data is used

Data is used only to answer the owner's messages and to send the owner reminders and summaries. It is never sold, shared for advertising, or used to contact anyone else. Messages from any number other than the owner's are ignored and not stored.

Service providers

Jarvis uses the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta) to send and receive messages, a server hosted in the European Union, and an AI model provider to draft replies. These providers process data only on Jarvis Labs' behalf and under their own terms.

Retention and deletion

Conversation data is kept only as long as needed to provide the service and can be deleted at any time on request in the WhatsApp chat. Requests to delete data received through Meta are handled the same way.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent as a message to Jarvis's WhatsApp business number.
